# sikm-lqs

I’m a graduate student at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, exploring **LLM agents, agentic reinforcement learning, and the algorithms behind large language models**.

I follow my curiosity through papers and small experiments. On my blog, I share reading notes and questions I’m still thinking through.

[Blog](https://myweb-649.pages.dev/) · [Bilibili](https://space.bilibili.com/563285166?spm_id_from=333.1007.0.0)

## Learning and experiments

- **[Qwen2.5 Post-Training](https://github.com/sikm-lqs/grpo_qwen2.5)** · Training scripts and notes for SFT, DPO, PPO, and GRPO, with evaluation code for GSM8K.
- **[Build GPT-2](https://github.com/sikm-lqs/Build-GPT2)** · A from-scratch implementation of GPT-2 covering pretraining, fine-tuning, and text generation.
- **[Reinforcement Learning on CartPole](https://github.com/sikm-lqs/RL-demo-Cartpole)** · Implementations of DQN, policy gradient, actor–critic, and PPO on CartPole.
